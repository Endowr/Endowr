Questions

A few things people ask

Do we need to change our current ATS or software?+
No. We work alongside whatever you already use. Your ATS stays exactly as it is. We simply take on the day-to-day tracking and chasing that currently falls to your team.
What does the pilot actually cost?+
The pilot is offered at a significantly reduced rate, in exchange for close collaboration and honest feedback while we shape the service around what actually works. Exact terms are agreed individually. Get in touch and we'll talk it through.
Which sectors do you work with?+
We work with recruitment and staffing agencies across regulated sectors, including healthcare, construction, education, and security. Anywhere placed workers need right-to-work, DBS, or licence renewals kept current.
Do you check whether our documents are legally valid?+
No. We track, flag, and chase, but we don't make legal or compliance judgement calls on your behalf. That decision always sits with you and your agency. Our job is making sure nothing gets missed or forgotten.
Does this guarantee we'll never face a compliance issue?+
No service can promise that, and we won't pretend otherwise. What we do is substantially reduce the risk of something slipping through, and put you in a far stronger position if you're ever audited.
