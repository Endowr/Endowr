How it works

Handover. Track. Chase. Escalate.

A deliberately narrow scope. We start the moment a worker is already live on your system, and we stay out of your onboarding process entirely.

01
Step one

Handover

Once your team's onboarded a worker (contracts, bank details, induction all done), their file is simply passed to us. We're not part of that first step at all.

02
Step two

Track

We keep a live register of what's on file and what's missing or expiring: right-to-work documents, DBS certificates, licences, references. We flag gaps straight back to you.

03
Step three

Chase

We chase the worker directly for renewals, follow up until we get a response, and log everything back into your system, so your files stay current without your team lifting a finger.

04
Step four

Escalate

If a worker stays unresponsive, or if something needs a judgement call, we bring it straight back to you. We don't make legal or compliance decisions on your behalf, ever.

About Endowr

A new, focused company, built by talking to agencies first.

Endowr is an early-stage company. We're not a large, established vendor, and we're not going to pretend otherwise, we're a small, focused team building a specific service, from the ground up, by speaking directly with recruitment agencies about a problem they actually have.

That's a deliberate starting point. Rather than building a broad platform and hoping it fits, we started with real conversations about how compliance chasing actually works day to day, what falls through the cracks, and what agencies would genuinely value. The pilot programme is a continuation of that approach: working closely with a small number of agencies to shape the service around what actually helps, before scaling it further.

We think that's a better way to build something worth relying on, even if it means starting smaller and moving more carefully than a bigger company might.

Questions

A few things people ask

Do we need to change our current ATS or software?+
No. We work alongside whatever you already use. Your ATS stays exactly as it is. We simply take on the day-to-day tracking and chasing that currently falls to your team.
What does the pilot actually cost?+
The pilot is offered at a significantly reduced rate, in exchange for close collaboration and honest feedback while we shape the service around what actually works. Exact terms are agreed individually. Get in touch and we'll talk it through.
Which sectors do you work with?+
We work with recruitment and staffing agencies across regulated sectors, including healthcare, construction, education, and security. Anywhere placed workers need right-to-work, DBS, or licence renewals kept current.
Do you check whether our documents are legally valid?+
No. We track, flag, and chase, but we don't make legal or compliance judgement calls on your behalf. That decision always sits with you and your agency. Our job is making sure nothing gets missed or forgotten.
Does this guarantee we'll never face a compliance issue?+
No service can promise that, and we won't pretend otherwise. What we do is substantially reduce the risk of something slipping through, and put you in a far stronger position if you're ever audited.
